## The situation

Basketball stats sites read like a tax return: tables, filters, more tables. We wanted 46 seasons of NBA history you explore by moving through it, not by sorting columns.

## The system

- The data: NBA seasons from 1980 to 2025, and 6.1 million shots from play-by-play (1996 – 2024), processed in Python from 1.6 GB of raw files.
- A map of teams and players on the metrics you choose, with a time slider that animates the seasons.
- Team and player dashboards (radars, matchups, game-by-game curves), an interactive court, a playoffs mode and the awards. All in D3.js and plain JavaScript.

## Results

46 seasons, about 6,200 players, 55,000 games and 6.1 million shots, in one site that runs in the browser.
